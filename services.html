Our Programs

Counseling, pastoral care, hospitality, and training — woven together into one ministry of healing. Tap a program below to see the details.

01Christian Counseling
Licensed clinical counseling, integrated with a Christian faith perspective
+

Licensed clinical counseling for individuals, couples, and families, with a Christian faith perspective woven through the work. This is clinical care — delivered by licensed mental health counselors, bound by the same confidentiality and clinical standards as any counseling practice — with scripture, prayer, and faith available as part of the process.

  • Individual, couples, and family counseling
  • Group therapy for trauma recovery, grief, and anxiety
  • Faith-integrated, evidence-based clinical care
  • Sliding scale payment options and scholarships
02Pastoral Care
Prayer, spiritual direction & biblical encouragement — not clinical therapy
+

Pastoral care is spiritual support, not clinical treatment. It's time with one of our pastors for prayer, spiritual direction, and biblical encouragement — walking alongside you in faith. It doesn't diagnose or treat a condition, and it isn't a substitute for licensed counseling, though the two often work well side by side.

  • Pastoral counseling and prayer ministry
  • Spiritual direction and biblical encouragement
  • Devotional journaling and scriptural healing groups
  • Provided by pastors, not a replacement for clinical care
03Non-Faith-Based Counseling
The same licensed clinical care, without a religious lens
+

Not everyone who needs support shares our faith, and that's okay — you're still welcome at this table. This is the same licensed clinical counseling as our Christian Counseling track, offered by the same counselors, just without scripture or prayer unless you ask for it. Professional mental health care, no faith requirement.

  • Individual, couples, and family counseling
  • Group therapy for trauma recovery, grief, and anxiety
  • Delivered by licensed counselors, without a religious framework
  • Sliding scale payment options and scholarships
04Pastor to Pastor Counseling
Confidential support and counseling for pastors and ministry leaders
+

Pastors carry weight that's hard to set down in front of their own congregation. This service offers a confidential space for pastors and ministry leaders in Baton Rouge and the surrounding areas to be cared for themselves — led by one of our pastoral counselors who understands the unique pressures of ministry leadership.

  • One-on-one, confidential pastor-to-pastor counseling
  • Support for burnout, conflict, grief, and the weight of leadership
  • Serving pastors, ministry leaders, and their spouses in Baton Rouge and surrounding areas
05Therapeutic Cooking Workshops
Trauma-informed culinary therapy and soul care classes
+

Cooking becomes a healing practice — a way to process, connect, and pass down stories between generations.

  • Trauma-informed culinary therapy
  • Nutrition and soul care classes
  • Intergenerational cooking and storytelling circles
06Community Meals & Events
Monthly dinners, pop-up meals & holiday feasts
+

Monthly dinners and pop-up meals bring healing conversation to the neighborhoods that need it most.

  • Monthly “Healing Table” dinners with topical discussions
  • Pop-up meals in underserved communities
  • Holiday feasts with devotional messages
07Donation-Only Restaurant & Coffee Shop
A “pay what you can” café staffed in part by youth trainees
+

A “pay what you can” restaurant and café open to the public, staffed in part by our youth trainees.

  • Operated as a hospitality ministry, not a business
  • Open to the public and staffed in part by youth trainees
  • Profits reinvested into ministry operations and scholarships

Coming in Phase 2, as part of our facility expansion.

08Youth & Young Adult Training Program
Culinary, hospitality & life skills training with mentorship
+

Practical culinary and hospitality training combined with discipleship and life skills — equipping the next generation for work and for life.

  • Culinary, hospitality, and customer service training
  • Life skills, financial literacy, and discipleship
  • Mentorship from Christian leaders and licensed counselors
