Our Programs

Counseling, pastoral care, hospitality, and training — woven together into one ministry of healing.

Counseling

Faith-Based Counseling

Licensed, Christ-centered counseling for individuals, couples, and families — because clinical excellence and compassionate care belong together.

  • Individual, couples, and family counseling
  • Group therapy for trauma recovery, grief, and anxiety
  • Sliding scale payment options and scholarships
💬

Who It's For

Anyone navigating emotional or spiritual struggles who needs affordable, faith-based support — regardless of their ability to pay.

🙏

Rooted in Prayer

Spiritual direction, biblical encouragement, and devotional journaling walk alongside clinical care.

Pastoral Care

Pastoral Counseling & Prayer Ministry

For those seeking spiritual encouragement alongside emotional healing.

  • Pastoral counseling and prayer ministry
  • Spiritual direction and biblical encouragement
  • Devotional journaling and scriptural healing groups
Culinary Ministry

Therapeutic Cooking Workshops

Cooking becomes a healing practice — a way to process, connect, and pass down stories between generations.

  • Trauma-informed culinary therapy
  • Nutrition and soul care classes
  • Intergenerational cooking and storytelling circles
🍳

Healing Through Making

Working with our hands, together, creates space for conversation and connection that talk therapy alone can't always reach.

🍴

Gather Around the Table

No one leaves a Healing Table dinner without a full plate, a good conversation, and a reminder they're not alone.

Community

Community Meals & Events

Monthly dinners and pop-up meals bring healing conversation to the neighborhoods that need it most.

  • Monthly “Healing Table” dinners with topical discussions
  • Pop-up meals in underserved communities
  • Holiday feasts with devotional messages
Hospitality

Donation-Only Restaurant & Coffee Shop

A “pay what you can” restaurant and café open to the public, staffed in part by our youth trainees.

  • Operated as a hospitality ministry, not a business
  • Open to the public and staffed in part by youth trainees
  • Profits reinvested into ministry operations and scholarships
☕

Coming in Phase 2

Our donation-based café and counseling center opens as part of our facility expansion — a home base for the whole ministry.

🌱

Skills for Life

Mentorship from Christian leaders and licensed counselors, paired with real job training.

Training

Youth & Young Adult Training Program

Practical culinary and hospitality training combined with discipleship and life skills — equipping the next generation for work and for life.

  • Culinary, hospitality, and customer service training
  • Life skills, financial literacy, and discipleship
  • Mentorship from Christian leaders and licensed counselors
