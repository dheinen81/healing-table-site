Frequently Asked

Answers to what people usually ask before reaching out.

Do I have to be a Christian to receive counseling or attend a dinner?
+

No. Our doors are open to anyone seeking support, regardless of faith background. Ask for Non-Faith-Based Counseling if you'd rather your sessions not include scripture or prayer — it's the same licensed clinical care, just without the religious lens. Everyone has a seat at the table.

What's the difference between Christian Counseling, Non-Faith-Based Counseling, and Pastoral Care?
+

Christian Counseling and Non-Faith-Based Counseling are both licensed clinical care from the same counselors — the only difference is whether faith is woven into your sessions. Pastoral Care is different: it's spiritual support from one of our pastors (prayer, spiritual direction, biblical encouragement), not clinical treatment, and it's not a substitute for licensed counseling. Many people use both together.

How much does counseling cost?
+

We offer sliding-scale payment options and scholarships, so cost is never a barrier to getting care. Ask us about what's available when you reach out.

Is my counseling confidential?
+

Yes. Our licensed counselors follow standard confidentiality and clinical supervision protocols, and we carry liability insurance to support that care.

I'm a pastor — how does Pastor to Pastor Counseling work?
+

This service offers confidential, one-on-one counseling for pastors and ministry leaders in Baton Rouge and the surrounding areas — a space to be cared for yourself, outside your own congregation. Reach out through the form above and mention Pastor to Pastor Counseling in your message.

How do I join a community dinner?
+

Reach out through the form above or by email, and we'll let you know the date, time, and location of our next Healing Table dinner. No strings attached — just come as you are.

Is the café or community pantry open yet?
+

Not yet — the café, teaching kitchen, and community pantry open together as part of our Phase 2 expansion. The pantry will offer groceries and household essentials to anyone in need, no questions asked. Reach out to be added to our list and hear first when it opens.

Can my church partner with The Healing Table?
+

Absolutely. We partner with churches for speaking engagements, co-hosted community meals, and scholarship sponsorships. Visit our Get Involved page or contact us to start the conversation.

Are donations tax-deductible?
+

Yes. The Healing Table is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, and every gift is tax-deductible.

How can a young person join the training program?
+

Our Youth & Young Adult Training Program launches alongside our Phase 2 facility expansion. Contact us to be added to the interest list so you're first to know when applications open.

Can we use your space for a support group or event?
+

That's the plan — our trauma-informed event space opens as part of our Phase 2 facility expansion, available for community partners, outside organizations, and support groups. Reach out to be added to our list and hear first when it's ready.
