About The Healing Table

A nonprofit Christian counseling and hospitality ministry that blends licensed mental health care, pastoral support, and therapeutic community meals.

Mission Statement

“To bring healing to hurting hearts through faith-based counseling, hospitality, and the ministry of presence around the table.”

Vision Statement

“To build a community where people are restored in mind, renewed in spirit, and reminded of their God-given worth through the simple act of gathering at the table. To become a beacon of hope, healing, and hospitality that empowers individuals, strengthens families, and inspires churches to care for the whole person.”

02Our Story

Rooted in the belief that healing happens both in the heart and at the table

The Healing Table exists to serve individuals, couples, families, and young people through Christ-centered counseling, shared meals, and real-life training opportunities. Alongside our core ministry, we're building a donation-only restaurant and coffee shop — a training and mentoring facility where youth and young adults learn culinary and life skills in a supportive, faith-based environment.

Individuals & Families

We serve people facing emotional or spiritual struggles who need affordable, faith-based counseling.

Churches

We partner with churches seeking to care for the whole person and support community outreach.

Pastors & Ministry Leaders

Confidential, pastor-to-pastor counseling for those in Baton Rouge and the surrounding areas carrying the weight of ministry leadership.

Youth & Young Adults

We provide job training, mentorship, and a path toward stability and purpose.

03Where We're Headed

Our Growth Plan

The Healing Table is growing in three phases, from a small counseling and dinner ministry into a flagship center that can be replicated in other cities.

Phase 1 · Startup (Year 1)

Laying the Foundation

  • Incorporate as a 501(c)(3)
  • Build our board of directors and advisory team
  • Secure shared space for counseling and pop-up meals
  • Launch counseling services and pilot dinners
Phase 2 · Expansion (Years 2–3)

Building the Table

  • Lease or renovate a permanent facility
  • Open our donation-based café and counseling center
  • Launch the youth training program
  • Hire licensed staff and hospitality trainers
Phase 3 · Sustainability (Year 4+)

A Replicable Model

  • Operate the full Healing Table Center
  • Offer mobile outreach and satellite pop-ups
  • Launch internships and training replicable in other cities
  • Publish curriculum and build national partnerships
