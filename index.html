01Our Mission

Healing happens at the table.

We believe healing happens both in the heart and at the table. Through Christ-centered counseling, shared meals, and real-life training opportunities, we serve individuals, couples, families, and young people who are looking for restoration, community, and hope.

02What We Do

A ministry of counseling, hospitality, and training

Every part of The Healing Table works together — clinical care, pastoral support, shared meals, and hands-on training — to restore the whole person.

Christian Counseling

Licensed clinical counseling for individuals, couples, families, and groups, integrated with a Christian faith perspective, with sliding scale options and scholarships.

Pastoral Care

Prayer, spiritual direction, and biblical encouragement with one of our pastors — spiritual support, not clinical therapy.

Non-Faith-Based Counseling

The same licensed clinical counseling, offered without a religious lens — professional mental health support, no faith required.

Pastor to Pastor Counseling

Confidential counseling and support for pastors and ministry leaders in Baton Rouge and the surrounding areas.

Community Meals

Monthly Healing Table dinners, pop-up meals in underserved communities, and holiday feasts with devotional messages.

Donation-Only Café

A “pay what you can” restaurant and coffee shop staffed in part by youth trainees, reinvesting profits into ministry.

Therapeutic Cooking

Trauma-informed culinary workshops, nutrition and soul care classes, and intergenerational cooking and storytelling circles.

Youth & Young Adult Training

Culinary, hospitality, and customer service training paired with life skills, financial literacy, and Christian mentorship.

03Our Story

Rooted in the belief that healing happens both in the heart and at the table

The Healing Table began with a simple conviction: people heal in the presence of others, not in isolation. We blend licensed mental health care, pastoral support, and therapeutic community meals into one ministry — and we're building a donation-only restaurant and coffee shop that trains youth and young adults in culinary and life skills along the way.

Who We Serve

  • Individuals and families facing emotional or spiritual struggles
  • Those with limited access to affordable counseling, whether or not they share our faith
  • Churches seeking to partner in soul care and community outreach
  • Pastors and ministry leaders in Baton Rouge and surrounding areas who need confidential support of their own
  • Youth and young adults in need of job training and mentorship
