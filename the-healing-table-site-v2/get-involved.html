Support the Ministry

Your gift helps fund counseling scholarships, community meals, and youth training. The Healing Table is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and every gift is tax-deductible.

Monthly Giving

Become a monthly donor and provide steady, reliable support that lets us plan ahead and grow sustainably.

One-Time Gift

Make a one-time donation toward counseling scholarships, community dinners, or our building fund.

In-Kind & Sponsorship

Community Dinners

Mentorship

Licensed counselors, culinary professionals, and Christian leaders are needed to mentor our youth trainees.

Behind the Scenes

Partner

Church & Community Partnerships

We partner with churches and organizations who want to care for the whole person — mind, body, and spirit. Partnership can look like hosting a pop-up meal, sponsoring a counseling scholarship, or inviting us to speak.

  • Church partnerships and speaking engagements
  • Co-hosted community meals and outreach events
  • Scholarship and sponsorship partnerships
